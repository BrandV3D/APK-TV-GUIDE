TV Movies Channel Guide - Setup Instructions
=============================================

1. Replace the empty "TV GUIDE" folder next to this app with your own
   "TV GUIDE" folder on your hard drive (or copy your movies into this one).

2. Put your videos inside the numbered Channel folders (Channel 1, Channel 2,
   etc.) - one channel's movies per folder.

3. For each channel's Schedual folder, drop the .mp4 files you want that
   channel to air. The app reads files from both the channel folder itself
   and its Schedual subfolder, and schedules them into a repeating 24-hour
   daily lineup, shuffled randomly.

4. Edit LinkToRoot.txt so it contains the full path to your TV GUIDE folder's
   root, e.g. J:\FandangoMovies\Movies\TVGUIDE

That's it - launch the app and it will scan that folder and build the guide.
